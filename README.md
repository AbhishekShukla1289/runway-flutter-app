# Runway 👗✨  
*A Fashion-First Microblogging App built with Flutter*

Runway is a clean, community-powered microblogging platform where fashion designers, stylists, and trendseekers can connect, discover inspiration, and share style-forward ideas. The app is crafted using Flutter to deliver a seamless, cross-platform user experience with elegant UI and smooth performance.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Personalized Feed**  
  View latest posts from the designers and creators you follow.

- **Explore Page**  
  Discover trending designs, latest looks, and curated content from across the fashion community.

- **User Profile**  
  Display your portfolio, including your posts, profile picture, followers, and following stats.

- **Post Detail View**  
  Tap on any post to view comments, insights, and likes to engage with the community more deeply.

- **Like, Comment & Engage**  
  Share your thoughts, show support, and exchange creative ideas with like-minded fashion enthusiasts.
